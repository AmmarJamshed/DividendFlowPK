# DividendFlow.pk — Partner Affiliate Runbook (No Desk)

**Purpose:** Run **dividendflow.pk** as a PSX **affiliate / introducer** under a licensed broker — **no physical desk**, no TREC, no SECP broker licence.

**Partnership story:** DividendFlow brings **dividend-focused retail** through education and tools → clients open accounts at the **partner broker** → broker earns full client relationship + brokerage; DividendFlow earns an agreed **introducer share**.

## 2. Why a broker partners with DividendFlow

Lead with value for the broker — not fee math.

- **Warm dividend audience** already engaging calendars, yields, and PSX news on dividendflow.pk
- **Digital acquisition only** — no desk, no branch cost for the broker
- **Broker keeps the client relationship**, platform, custody, and majority of economics
- **Tracked referrals** — clean attribution via code / UIN / deep link
- **Compliance-friendly** — DividendFlow does not take client funds or execute trades

## 3. What DividendFlow is (and is not)

### Is
- Content / tools / referral funnel for PSX dividend investors
- Introducer partner of one licensed broker
- Paid an agreed share of brokerage on referred activity

### Is not (this phase)
- SECP-licensed securities broker or TREC holder
- Customer Help Center / desk (Chapter 22A)
- Holder of client money or securities

**Legal note:** Stick to education, tools, and broker referral. Paid investment advice may need a Securities Advisor licence.  
Sources: [SECP Brokers](https://www.secp.gov.pk/licensing/capital-markets/agents-and-brokers/), [SECP Advisors](https://www.secp.gov.pk/licensing/capital-markets/securities-advisors-and-futures-advisor/)

## 5. Process to go live

### Step 1 — Shortlist brokers
Contact Retail / BD at active TREC brokers (KTrade, AKD, JS Global, Arif Habib, MMK, Standard Capital, etc.).  
List: [PSX select broker](https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/resources-and-tools/investors/select-your-stock-broker)

**Ask:**
1. Online affiliates / introducers with **no office**?
2. Introducer share & payout frequency?
3. Referral tracking (code / UIN)?
4. Deep-link from dividendflow.pk to account opening?
5. Exclusive vs non-exclusive; client ownership on exit?

### Step 2 — Agreement essentials
- [ ] Share % and calculation base  
- [ ] Payout schedule / minimum  
- [ ] Attribution rules  
- [ ] Marketing claims (no return guarantees)  
- [ ] Prohibited acts (no client funds, no trade execution)  
- [ ] Branding (DividendFlow vs broker)  
- [ ] Term, notice, exit / client ownership  
- [ ] Tax / invoicing  

### Step 3 — Onboard
- [ ] Docs + payout bank details  
- [ ] KYC/AML + product training  
- [ ] Referral code live  
- [ ] One test account open end-to-end  

### Step 4 — Wire the funnel
- [ ] CTA: “Open PSX account via our partner”  
- [ ] Tracked links on all CTAs  
- [ ] Funnel: click → opened → first trade  
- [ ] Disclaimer footer (§9)  

### Step 5 — Operate & get paid
Refer only. Accounts open on **broker** flow. Broker remits introducer share; reconcile monthly against referral report.

## 6. How the money works (keep light)

PSX Ready brokerage sits on a published floor (commonly **0.15% or 3 paisa**, whichever higher — buy/sell rules apply). Exact retail rates and **introducer share** are **negotiated with the partner**.

**Partner framing (use this in broker conversations):**
- Broker keeps platform, relationship, and the **majority** of brokerage
- DividendFlow is paid only for **attributed referred activity**
- No desk, no branch, no fixed rent from DividendFlow’s side
- Details live in the commercial schedule — not in marketing decks

Internal note: model payouts privately after a verbal/written split; do **not** lead partner pitches with break-even volume tables.

Source for floor: [PSX commission notice](https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/themes/psx/uploads/N-1026-GIS-Brokerage-Commission-and-Trading-Fee-Oc-24-2024.pdf)

## 8. SECP & PSX guidelines (affiliate / introducer)

There is **no separate SECP “affiliate broker” licence**. Brokerage to the public requires a **PSX TREC + SECP securities broker licence** under the Securities Act, 2015 and the Securities Brokers (Licensing and Operations) Regulations, 2016. ([SECP Brokers](https://www.secp.gov.pk/licensing/capital-markets/agents-and-brokers/))

The old standalone agent regime (*Brokers and Agents Registration Rules, 2001*) was **repealed** (referenced in the PSX Rule Book). Affiliates work **under a licensed broker** via commercial agreement.

| Path | When | Key filings |
|------|------|-------------|
| **A — Digital introducer (our model)** | No desk | Written referral agreement; broker training/control; accounts open only on broker platform; no trade execution by introducer |
| **B — Account Facilitation Center** | Physical help desk | Broker intimates PSX under **Chapter 22A**: undertaking, staff list, Designated Entity agreement, non-permissible activities ([PSX CFC](https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/resources-and-tools/brokerage-firms/account-facilitation-customer-help-center)) |

**Ch. 22A red lines (if Center used):** no trading/advisory at Center; no trading terminal; no cash dealing; broker remains liable for Designated Employees. ([PSX Rule Book Ch. 22A](https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/themes/psx/uploads/PSX-Rulebook-July-14-2025.pdf))

Paid investment advice as a business may need a separate **Securities Advisor** licence. ([SECP Advisors](https://www.secp.gov.pk/licensing/capital-markets/securities-advisors-and-futures-advisor/))

## 9. Forbidden actions

1. Calling DividendFlow a stock broker / brokerage house  
2. Collecting client trading funds  
3. Executing or clearing trades yourself  
4. Guaranteeing profits or yields  
5. Taking orders outside broker process  
6. Operating client accounts without proper broker authority
