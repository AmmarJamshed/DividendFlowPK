Scene · IPO · SECP / PSX guidelines

8 · SECP & PSX guidelines

No separate “affiliate broker” licence — operate under the partner’s licence.

RulePractical takeaway
SECP securities broker licenceOnly TREC + SECP licensed brokers offer brokerage to the public. We refer into theirs.
Old Agents Rules 2001Repealed — no standalone affiliate registration form.
Path A (us)Digital introducer, no desk; broker trains/controls; no trade execution by us.
Path B (optional)PSX Chapter 22A CFC if a physical help center is needed — broker files with PSX.
Ch. 22A red lines if a Center is used: no advisory/trading at Center, no trading terminal, no cash dealing; broker remains liable. Sources: SECP · PSX CFC · Rule Book Ch. 22A

9 · Forbidden actions

Protect the partnership and the brand.
