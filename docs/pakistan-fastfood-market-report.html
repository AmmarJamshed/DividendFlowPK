60-second executive snapshot (Simple Word answer)

Pakistan already loves fast food. Local shops + world brands share one big eating-out pot of about $6–9 billion a year. Opening a shop is exciting but hard (papers, rent, app fees). Government candy is stronger for food factories / SEZs than for a single fryer shop. Best “new but familiar” foods: crispy chicken, burgers, pizza, shawarma, Korean fried chicken. The emptier gold mine: smart food (health plans, school lunch, gym macros, trust meat) that Foodpanda-style apps are not built to run.

3. Simple economics (who pays whom)

Tech Expert + AI Market Buddy:

A burger shop is a money machine with pipes. Money comes in from hungry people. Money goes out to chicken, flour, rent, staff, apps, tax, and electricity. What is left is your “smile money” (profit). If pipes leak too much, smile goes away.

Money coming in

  • Dine-in tickets (family nights, after school)
  • Takeaway / drive-thru
  • App delivery (Foodpanda & friends)
  • Corporate / event catering
  • Franchise royalties (for brand owners)

Money going out

  • Food cost (biggest pipe for fried chicken & beef)
  • Rent in DHA / Gulberg / Clifton = high
  • Staff wages + training
  • App commission historically ~15–28%+ — PIDE 2022 / Profit 2020
  • Sales tax, municipal fees, utilities, packaging

4. Hard doors — barriers to entry

Focused Learner says:

Rockets look cool. Rockets also need fuel, papers, and a clear launch pad. Opening a food brand in Pakistan is the same: exciting, but the door has many locks.

For local founders

  • Many licenses / food authority rules / municipal NOCs — 2022 PIDE
  • Hard bank loans for first-time food SMEs
  • Finding trained staff is hard; hotels/restaurants often train in-house — PIDE 2022
  • Rent + fit-out cost in prime malls
  • Delivery apps may take a big bite of sales
  • Brand trust vs KFC / McD already living in people’s minds

For foreign brands

  • Need a strong local franchise partner (most big QSRs use this model)
  • Halal supply chain must be rock solid (almost default expectation)
  • Menu must be localized (spice, portions, family deals)
  • Import duties / forex for proprietary ingredients & royalties
  • Political / security / policy uncertainty (classic PIDE hotel barrier theme)
  • Competition already dense in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad

2022 PIDE Knowledge Brief No. 76 is the clearest public barriers story for hotels/restaurants: paperwork, weak skills pipeline, infrastructure gaps, and limited government responsiveness. PDF: direct PDF download.

5. Goodies & invites — incentives to enter

Motivated Investor says:

Pakistan does wave some candy for food investors — especially if you make food in a factory / SEZ, not only if you fry chicken in a shop. Factory candy ≠ shop candy. Know the difference.

For local + foreign (often shared)

  • Huge hungry market: 256k+ restaurants prove demand — Oct 2025 ProPakistani
  • Halal-by-default culture: 2023 Bonafide Halal F&B
  • Delivery rails already built: healthier dining demand rising — Statista Platform Delivery Pakistan
  • Franchise culture open to U.S. brands: ~70% U.S. franchise share — U.S. Trade.gov

Board of Investment — Food Processing goodies

  • Low customs duties on many capital goods for food processing — BOI incentives page
  • Concessional duties on ingredients for Value Added Chicken Products (VACP) — BOI
  • Sales tax exemptions on some livestock / meat related flows — BOI
  • Food processing highlighted as priority developmental sector — BOI Food Processing
  • Historical average FDI into food processing ~$223.5m/year (2012–2018) — BOI sector brief

Special Economic Zones (SEZ) — big candy for factories

  • One-time customs duty exemption on capital goods for zone developers / enterprises — BOI SEZ incentives
  • Income tax exemption up to 10 years for qualifying zone developers / enterprises — BOI SEZ incentives (check current SEZ Act text before relying on this)

Incentive hubs: invest.gov.pk/food-processing · SEZ incentives · Food processing & beverage incentives · Incentives database

6. What sells in the world — and what could sell here (ML style)

AI Market Buddy:

“I scan. You decide.” I did not secretly train on private Foodpanda receipts. I used a transparent content-based recommender score (like Netflix’s ‘similar tastes’ idea, but for food traits). Simple Word answer: every food idea gets points for Pakistan fit.

Models we used (named clearly)

  1. Content-Based Filtering (CBF) — recommend items whose features match a user/culture profile.
  2. Weighted Multi-Criteria Scoring (MCDA-style linear model) — same math as a simple ranking engine.
  3. Heuristic Collaborative Analogy — “markets that love X also love Y” using public global trends (burgers, pizza, fried chicken lead globally — Precedence / MarketDataForecast).
  4. Not used: deep neural nets, transformer LLMs as score engines, or paid POS datasets. (Honesty > fake AI magic.)

Score formula (how it was calculated)

ItemFitScore = 0.25 * HalalFit + 0.18 * ProteinMatch (chicken/beef vs pork) + 0.15 * SpiceAffinity + 0.12 * GlobalMomentum (world growth signal 0–1) + 0.10 * FamilyShareFit + 0.10 * DeliveryFit + 0.10 * PriceValueFit − 0.15 * ComplexityBarrier (hard cold-chain / rare skill / import-heavy) Then clamp to 0–100: Final = round(100 * max(0, min(1, ItemFitScore)))

Worked example (Korean fried chicken, halal): HalalFit=1, ProteinMatch=0.95, SpiceAffinity=0.9, GlobalMomentum=0.8, FamilyShareFit=0.85, DeliveryFit=0.9, PriceValueFit=0.7, ComplexityBarrier=0.35

0.25*1 + 0.18*0.95 + 0.15*0.9 + 0.12*0.8 + 0.10*0.85 + 0.10*0.9 + 0.10*0.7 − 0.15*0.35 = 0.25 + 0.171 + 0.135 + 0.096 + 0.085 + 0.09 + 0.07 − 0.0525 = 0.8445 → Final ≈ 84 / 100

7. Smart food business potential — gaps ecommerce / quick commerce miss

Informed Pro says:

Foodpanda / Careem-style apps are amazing at “I’m hungry in 30 minutes.” They are weaker at “please feed my diabetes plan for 30 days,” or “school lunch that is safe and tracked,” or “corporate gym macros every weekday.” That empty space is smart food.

Statista — Platform Delivery Pakistan already notes rising demand for healthier dining and culturally relevant options among young urbanites — while classic apps still skew toward known QSR comfort food.

8. Tiny playbook — local vs foreign entry

Local founder path

  • Start with one hero item scoring 80+ on our ML board
  • Own delivery for nearby radius; use apps as extra pipe
  • Publish calories / allergens — become the “honest” brand
  • Expand to meal-prep subscription before opening 10 stores
  • If manufacturing sauces / frozen, check BOI / SEZ candy

Foreign brand path

  • Pick a franchisee who already understands FBR + food authorities
  • Halal audit before marketing splash
  • Localize spice + family buckets on day one
  • Price ladder: value line + premium line
  • Use CPEC / SEZ only if you also process / package, not only retail
