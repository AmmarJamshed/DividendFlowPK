Stage 1 · Meet the company

Who is Engro Holdings?

Anime guide says

“Think of Engro Holdings as a big money backpack. Inside the backpack are many smaller businesses. The backpack’s stock code on PSX is ENGROH.”

Engro Holdings is an investment holding company. That means it owns and steers other companies more than it “makes one product” itself.

Easy picture: Mom owns a shop group. One shop sells fertilizer. One makes plastic (PVC). One makes power. One owns phone towers. Mom’s name on the stock market = Engro Holdings.

What sits in the backpack?

Fertilizer

Helps farms grow food crops.

Power & mining

Makes electricity / digs energy fuel.

Towers (new big move)

Owns phone tower sites (Deodar deal).

Polymer, terminals, trading

Chemicals, gas/chemical storage, trading.

Public company or private?

Brokerage research like this is almost always for public / listed companies — because you can buy the share on PSX and the company must publish reports. Private companies usually do not get a public Buy/Sell note.

Stage 2 · The big idea

Why do analysts care about ENGROH?

Anime guide says

“Three simple reasons. One: cleaner ownership. Two: new tower cash machine. Three: headline profit can look scary — but ‘core’ profit (real day job) got stronger.”

  1. Cleaner structure (2025): Engro Corporation became a fully owned child company of Engro Holdings. Owners now keep more of the group’s earnings (about 100% vs ~40% before).
  2. Towers bet: They bought a large phone-tower set (~10,600 towers, Deodar). Goal = steady rent-like cash over years.
  3. Don’t trust the scary headline alone: In 1H 2026, total profit fell vs last year because last year had big one-time gains. Underneath, core profit for owners rose a lot (~79%).
Baby tip: When profit “falls” after a one-time gift last year, ask: “Did the real shop get weaker… or did the gift just not repeat?”

Where did 1H 2026 profit come from?

  • Power & mining: about PKR 16.6 bn (strong helper)
  • Fertilizer: about PKR 7.1 bn
  • Towers / connectivity: about PKR 3.9 bn
  • Polymer: about PKR 1.6 bn (was very hard in 2025)
Dividend vs buyback: Dividend = company sends you cash. Buyback = company buys its own shares from the market. Engro recently preferred buybacks while funding towers. Same family idea: return value — different shape.

Rough valuation (learning toy, not truth)

If you take FY25 core owners’ profit (~29 bn) and market cap (~319 bn), you get about 11× earnings. That is a simple “price tag / yearly earning” toy — real brokers also build a sum-of-parts model.

Stage 4 · Risks & final tip

What can go wrong — and the simple takeaway

Anime guide says

“Every hero story has boss fights. Know the bosses before you cheer the win.”

Watch-outs

Fertilizer price wars · PVC cycle pain · Tower deal execution · Tax/policy changes · Confusing one-time accounting

Nice-to-see next

More tenants on towers · Fertilizer margins heal · Polymer delta stays healthy · Clearer cash-return plan later

Illustrative desk call: Accumulate

In simple words: many analysts might like it on dips if they believe the tower cash and core businesses will compound — but they would ignore noisy headline EPS and wait for execution proof. This is education, not an order to buy.
