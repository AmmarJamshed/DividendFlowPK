Stage 1 · What is an IPO?

SE Fruits is “opening the shop door” to the public

Anime guide says

“IPO = Initial Public Offering. The company sells new shares to people like you. Your money helps the company grow. You become a tiny owner.”

SE Fruits and Vegetable Limited (formerly Shaheen Enterprises) packs and exports kinnow (oranges), mangoes, and is building a potato line. Home base: Bhalwal / Sargodha, Punjab — Pakistan’s citrus heartland. Export markets include Middle East, Far East, Central Asia, Russia belt, and more. It is described as export-oriented and Shariah-compliant.

Easy picture: A fruit-export factory needs cash to buy fruit from farmers, keep it cold, and ship it in refrigerated containers. The IPO is how they raise that cash from the stock market.

Almost 28 years old

Started 1998 as a partnership; became a company in 2025–26 for listing.

Public vs private

Before IPO = mostly private owners. After IPO = anyone can buy on PSX (once listed).
