Simple Word answer: Pakistan already has a large eating-out market (about $6–9 bn/year in public Trade.gov citations). Local + international brands share demand. Hard doors are licenses, rent, and delivery fees. Factory/SEZ incentives help processors more than single shops. Best familiar foods score high; smart gaps sit in planned nutrition and trust meals.

2. Economics

Drivers

  • Urban demand and convenience channels keep expanding in Pakistan consumer markets. — 2019 U.S. Trade.gov — Pakistan Franchising
  • Public investment / industry pages highlight food and related processing as a priority developmental area. — 2020 BOI Food Processing

Restraints

  • Documentation and operating frictions raise barriers for hospitality and restaurant-like businesses. — 2022 PIDE KB 76
  • Price sensitivity and thin margins compress growth when input costs or platform fees rise. — 2019 U.S. Trade.gov — Pakistan Franchising

3. Barriers to entry

  • Licenses, food-authority / municipal paperwork, and slow responses deter new operators. — 2022 PIDE
  • Skilled labor often needs in-house training. — 2022 PIDE

4. Incentives

  • BOI lists customs and related concessions for food processing capital goods and some value-added chicken inputs. — 2020 BOI incentives
  • SEZ framework offers capital-goods duty relief and multi-year income tax holidays for qualifying enterprises. — 2020 BOI SEZ
